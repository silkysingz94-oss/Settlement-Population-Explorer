04 · Reading the results

Population totals sum the numeric population field for the filtered source rows. Missing or non-numeric values are excluded from the sum.

The district chart sums the rows assigned to each district. It should not be interpreted as an official district total unless the source records and aggregation method have been validated.

If the CSV has coordinates, each valid row is mapped. Without coordinates, the map may show reference locations only; those locations are not linked to population records.

05 · Data quality checks

Check for missing settlement names, duplicate settlement codes, absent administrative fields, invalid coordinates, and missing population values before using results for official reporting.

Methodology

Source data is loaded from the configured CSV URL. Administrative filtering is performed in the browser. The original CSV rows are preserved; no population estimates or settlement coordinates are fabricated.

Population sums and means are descriptive statistics for the currently selected records. Their validity depends on the source population definition, reference period, and completeness.

Coordinate validity checks test latitude and longitude ranges. They cannot confirm that a coordinate is positioned at the correct settlement.
